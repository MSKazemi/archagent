How the 48-hour service works.

A practical service workflow for teams that need a fast, source-linked view of bid fit, risks, missing documents, and next actions.

step.01

Share the opportunity

Send one tender notice, CPV segment, target region, or active opportunity your team is considering.

step.02

We verify public sources

ArchAgent checks the official notice, deadlines, buyer, visible value, geography, and available documents.

step.03

We prepare the pack

The output includes a readable summary, checklist, compliance matrix, review questions, gaps, and recommended next action.

step.04

Human review before use

The pack is decision support. Eligibility, legal obligations, technical requirements, and final submission remain human-reviewed.

step.05

Continue only if useful

If the tender is worth pursuing, ArchAgent can support partner shortlists, clarification questions, or recurring tender monitoring.
