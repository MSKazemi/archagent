One workflow for the entire building project lifecycle.

ArchAgent is the blueprint for a BuildingOS: every step from discovering opportunities to designing, quoting, staffing, executing, inspecting, and handing over the work.

01 / DISCOVER
Find projects from EU tenders, permits, real estate, property managers, renovation requests, energy grants, and local demand.
Output: qualified project lead with location, budget, deadline, owner type, and required trades.
02 / AUDIT
Collect photos, floor plans, bills, building age, symptoms, area, climate, and existing materials.
Output: building health score, risks, missing data, and priority problems.
03 / DESIGN
Generate architecture concepts, renovation plans, facade ideas, interior options, before/after previews, and compliance checklist.
Output: design direction, drawings checklist, material palette, and project scope.
04 / MATERIALS
Recommend insulation, windows, paint, flooring, tiles, roofing, waterproofing, HVAC, solar, smart home, and safety materials.
Output: material options ranked by cost, durability, energy impact, supplier availability, and installation difficulty.
05 / MATCH
Match architects, engineers, painters, electricians, plumbers, HVAC experts, insulation installers, roofers, facade crews, and contractors.
Output: local expert shortlist, quote requests, schedules, portfolios, and risk score.
06 / PROPOSE
Generate scope of work, quote comparison, budget range, timeline, milestones, warranty assumptions, and payment schedule.
Output: client-ready proposal or contractor-ready bid package.
07 / EXECUTE
Coordinate calendar, tasks, materials, workers, site photos, client approvals, invoices, change orders, and milestone payments.
Output: live project dashboard for owner, expert, and admin.
08 / INSPECT
Use AI photo checks and human review for paint coverage, insulation installation, facade quality, site cleanliness, and before/after proof.
Output: inspection report, punch list, final approval, and maintenance reminders.
